# Rank & Badge Specification — V1

This specification is application/indexer-layer only. Rank never changes the frozen
50/43/2/5 payout percentages in V1. All inputs are deterministically reconstructable
from `UserRegistered` and `UnitsPurchased` on-chain events.

## Personal qualification versus rank

- Personal purchases control ACTIVE status and weekly network depth only.
- Personal purchases are excluded from the user's own QNV rank calculation.
- Claimed earnings are excluded from rank.
- QNV is gross purchase units generated by all descendants, grouped by immutable
  direct-referral leg, over a rolling 30-day window.
- A Qualified Leg must contribute at least 100 QNV units in the rolling 30-day window.
- Balance caps are applied to the QNV required for the rank, not to lifetime volume.

| Current Rank | QNV / rolling 30d | Qualified Legs | Maximum contribution from one leg |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Bronze | 1,000 | 2 | none |
| Silver | 5,000 | 2 | none |
| Gold | 15,000 | 3 | 60% |
| Platinum | 50,000 | 4 | 50% |
| Emerald | 150,000 | 5 | 50% |
| Diamond | 500,000 | 6 | 50% |
| Double Diamond | 1,500,000 | 6 | 45% |
| Crown | 5,000,000 | 8 | 40% |
| Crown Ambassador | 15,000,000 | 10 | 40% |

The UI exposes both `Current Rank` (rolling/dynamic) and `Highest Lifetime Rank`
(permanent historical achievement).

## Pioneer badge

Pioneer is independent of rank and is displayed as `PIONEER ×N`, where N is the
number of permanently-owned Pioneer positions. The global cap remains 100 positions,
not 100 wallets. Pioneer positions never recycle, but their economic rewards remain
ACTIVE-only under the core activity/GRACE/expiry rules.
